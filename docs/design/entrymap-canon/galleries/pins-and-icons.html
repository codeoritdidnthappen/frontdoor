EntryMap — pins & icons

The three evidence-tier pins and the app icon set, rebuilt as standalone inline SVG against board-refs/…/02-map-filters-and-evidence.png and 03-community-scan-flow.png. Every mark below is drawn by the two functions at the bottom of this page — the gallery renders from the same source it hands you, so what you see is what you paste.

Pins on a 42×52 body inside a −9 0 60 52 viewBox Icons on one 24px grid, stroke-width 2 round caps + joins aria-hidden, focusable=false no libraries · no data URIs · no external refs

1 · The three tiers on the map ground

Map size is 28 px tall; legends and empty states use 64 px. Distinguishable by shape alone: dashed open outline / solid teardrop with a segmented ring / solid teardrop with a detached tick badge. Colour is confirmation, never the carrier. Ratios are computed live from the drawn colours against the ground #EDEBFA.

2 · The anchor is the tip

The teardrop tip sits at exactly (21, 52) — bottom centre of the viewBox — so the app positions a pin by its point: left: x − width/2; top: y − height; with no per-tier fudge. The halo is allowed to bleed past the box via overflow:visible, which keeps the geometry honest without clipping the glow. The dashed line below is the anchor latitude; all three tips rest on it at both sizes.

anchor y

3 · Needs-match halos

A halo answers “does this fit the needs you told us about?” — never “is this place good?”. Both states use the same neutral purple, so the difference is carried by form: a continuous ring on a soft disc for a full match, an open, broken ring with no disc for a partial one. Nothing green, nothing amber, nothing red; no score, no stars. The adjacent label always says which needs, in words.

The halo never changes the pin inside it. Evidence tier and needs-fit are two independent axes, and merging them would make a pin look like a rating.

4 · Cluster

Zoomed out, dense pins collapse to a count. The cluster is deliberately not a teardrop — it is a disc, so it never reads as a fourth tier. Its dotted outer ring says “several things here”. It anchors at its centre, not at a tip.

The digits are inside aria-hidden SVG, so the surrounding control must carry the count in its accessible name — e.g. aria-label="12 entrances here, zoom in".

5 · Every pin again, on a white card

Pins also appear inside sheets, list rows and legends. The white halo disappears on white — which is correct; on white the tier colour itself is already past 3:1, so nothing is doing a job it does not need to do.

6 · The icon set

One 24 px grid, stroke-width 2 everywhere, round caps, round joins, fill="none", no filled counters. Colour arrives through currentColor, so the surrounding CSS custom property drives it and one drawing serves every tone. The build previously mixed 1.7 / 1.8 / 1.9 / 2 / 2.1 / 2.2 / 2.3 / 2.4 / 2.6 and drew several icons off-grid; all of that collapses here.

Colour comes from the token, not the drawing

7 · Drop-in source

Signatures match the prototype: pinSVG(tier, size) and iconSVG(name, size). Both take one optional trailing argument (opts for pins — match halo and cluster count; color for icons) that existing two-argument call sites never pass, so nothing at the call site has to change.

8 · Where this departs from the board, and why

  

    
BoardHereReason

    
      

        Scanned pin is bright marigold with only a white keyline around it.
        Same marigold, plus a #8A5A00 rim inside the white halo.
        Marigold alone measures  against the lavender ground and
            fails 1.4.11. White-on-lavender is 1.06:1, so the keyline carries nothing. The rim is
            the smallest change that keeps the hue and passes.
      

      

        Person glyph is dark ink, not white — visual-language §5 says “a white glyph inside”.
        Dark ink #241F3A, following the board.
        The board is right and the prose is wrong: white on marigold measures
            . Ink measures . The board's own
            rule is “highest-contrast neutral against the tier fill”, which is why the owner
            storefront is white on purple () and the scanned
            person is not.
      

      

        Legend pins carry a segmented ring outside the head; map pins carry it inside.
        Always inside.
        One drawing at both sizes. An outer ring at 28 px would collide with the halo and read
            as a match halo, which means something else entirely.
      

      

        The “i” is set in an italic serif face.
        Drawn as geometry: a dot over a slanted stem.
        No webfont is loadable inside a standalone SVG snippet, and a <text>
            element would re-flow with the user's font settings at 28 px.
      

      

        Halo is a blurred soft glow.
        Three stacked white strokes at falling width and rising opacity.
        Reads identically at 28–64 px and costs no filter, no <defs>, and no
            per-instance id — which matters when the map renders a hundred of them through
            innerHTML.
      

      

        Texas flag tucked beside the scanned pin on the map.
        Not reproduced.
        Product doc 2026-09-04 and visual-language §9: the flag is provenance on a receipt row
            only. It never touches a pin. Deliberately dropped.
