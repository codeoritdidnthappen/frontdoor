Estimated mark gallery 2

Second asset brief for the Estimated tier. The first gallery (estimated-mark-gallery.html) was rejected because every candidate was still a teardrop with a glyph in it; the owner asked for something else to show estimate. Three treatments here differ in kind, not in dressing: a mark that is not a pin (E), a pin with nothing inside (F), a pin whose fill is a texture (G). No confidence dots anywhere (D38). No glyphs. No dash or dotted stroke on any mark; the halos own those.

Every colour on this page is one of the canonical twelve from asset-library/tokens/variables.css. Pins are composed the way pinSVG composes a library asset: the asset drawing, under it the build's white lift strokes, under those the match halo (r 50 on the head centre, with the lavender disc that appears only in a match state). Scanned (marigold, indigo rim) and Owner (violet, white rim) are drawn as the build draws them today minus the dots. Sizes are the ladder in pin-hierarchy.json: Estimated 32 / 40 / 52, Scanned 36 / 44 / 56, Owner 40 / 48 / 60. The copyable source under each candidate is the asset alone, in the library's 96 × 104 frame.
