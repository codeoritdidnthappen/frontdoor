Estimated mark gallery 4 — the doorway glyph

Fourth asset-drawing brief for the Estimated tier. The owner has specified the pin-head glyph: a minimal doorway outline with a dotted threshold, two rounded vertical posts and one short lintel, three small dots across the open threshold, no door leaf, storefront, checkmark, question mark or warning symbol. This page draws exactly that glyph in sky700 and renders it in four settings, J1 to J4, so the owner can choose the setting rather than the drawing.

Every colour on this page is one of the canonical twelve from asset-library/tokens/variables.css. Pins are composed the way pinSVG in the build composes a library asset: the asset drawing, under it the build's white lift strokes, and under those the match halo (r 50 on the head centre, with the lavender disc that appears only in a match state). The copyable source under each setting is the asset alone, in the library's own 96 × 104 frame. No confidence dots are drawn above any head: the owner removed them as clutter, and the only three dots on this page are the ones in the threshold.

The glyph on its own

Posts at x 32 and 64, from y 22 to 60, round caps; lintel across the top at y 22, round joins into the posts; three dots of r 2.2 at x 40, 48, 56 on the threshold line (y 60) between the posts. Stroke 4, the library's pin outline weight, so at 32px the glyph line and the pin outline are the same 1.35 px. The bounding box is 36 × 42 including stroke, centred on the head centre (48, 41.3). One honest caveat from the 1× crops: the round caps on the post feet have a radius of 0.67 px at 32 and 0.84 px at 40, so a 1× pixel grid squares them off; the caps read as round from 52 up and on any 2× or 3× screen. What survives at 32 is that the posts and the lintel are three distinct strokes with an open threshold, which is the doorway.
